Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This notice explains how I handle personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) when you contact me through this website. I, Ansh Kumar, am the data fiduciary for information submitted through this site. You can contact me at anshworkingthisside@gmail.com.

Data I collect and why

The contact form collects your name, email address, and message so that I can review and respond to your enquiry. I process this data on the basis of your consent, given by submitting the form, for the specified purpose of responding to your enquiry.1

Providing this information is voluntary, but I cannot respond through the form without it. I do not use contact-form data for marketing or automated decision-making.

Service providers and transfers

Cloudflare provides hosting and abuse protection. Its Turnstile service processes technical and security signals, including your IP address and browser information, to distinguish legitimate users from automated traffic. Resend handles email delivery, and my email service provider receives and may store the resulting message. Cloudflare and Resend act as data processors on my behalf in connection with providing these services, and process data as reasonably necessary for website and form security and abuse prevention.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. Where that happens, it relies on the providers' own safeguards and applicable transfer mechanisms. You can contact me for further information about the providers involved.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiries only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, normally no longer than 12 months after our last correspondence. Relevant information may be kept longer if an enquiry leads to a professional relationship or where required for legal, contractual, or record-keeping purposes.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you may ask me to:

  • give you access to your personal data;
  • correct, complete, or update it;
  • erase it, once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was given;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it; or
  • nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. You can also raise a grievance with me directly, or with the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.

Cookieless analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless service, for aggregate traffic and performance analytics. It does not use analytics cookies, connect analytics to details submitted through the contact form, or build advertising profiles.
